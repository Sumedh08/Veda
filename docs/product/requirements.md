# Product requirements (initial MVP)

## MVP-core

- Authenticated users create and manage private research projects.
- A project starts a scoped, budget-limited research job whose durable stages can be advanced/resumed from the browser.
- A research question alone initiates bounded discovery across applicable scholarly and official source channels. Veda derives source classes from the question, discovers affiliation-linked organizations from scholarly records, verifies exact ROR identities where present, and records source classes that were not searched. Organization identity and reputation never establish publication quality.
- Each job records source discovery, access level, evidence passages, claims, appraisal unknowns, citation audit, and a saved report or explicit incomplete state.
- Users can inspect sources and passage provenance, see the actual job timeline, and export reports.
- If search or model credentials are missing, Veda says which stage did not run and offers the safe next step.
- Ownership is enforced by the backend, not by client filtering.

## Deferred

Unattended worker execution, paid web search, full PDF/table extraction, private organization documents, semantic memory, team roles, billing, broad source crawling, and validated economic evidence ratings. General web search and direct consulting, think-tank, and broad government-publication catalogs remain unavailable until an authorized provider/source connector is configured; their absence must remain visible in search coverage.

## Unsupported claims

Veda will not advertise scientific superiority, universal source credibility scores, validated firm ROI, guaranteed exact-once provider billing, or unattended completion on a sleeping free host without evidence.
