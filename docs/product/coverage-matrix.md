## Read this status carefully

- The automated deterministic workflow uses a fake Crossref response and local SQLite test DB. It proves selected persistence and integrity invariants, not that actual scholarly search is complete or that conclusions are accurate.
- The OpenRouter live smoke validated one non-sensitive structured-output request through Veda's provider adapter. It did not run a Veda research job or evaluate research quality.
- Deployment is prepared locally and remains unverified remotely. A publicly reachable app and cross-device persistence are not yet demonstrated.
