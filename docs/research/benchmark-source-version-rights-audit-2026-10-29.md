## Registry alignment and safe-use summary

- All six DOI targets identify the selected studies. For reproducible passage-level evaluation, distinguish the exact source versions above rather than treating “open” as a single access or reuse class.
- **Passage use under an explicitly stated license:** Becker v2, Dell’Acqua publisher version, and Otis publisher version state CC BY 4.0; retain title/authors/DOI, link the license, and mark changes. Brynjolfsson’s publisher article states CC BY-NC 4.0; passage use must remain noncommercial with attribution, and Veda’s applicable use has not been classified here.
- **Metadata/independent notes only pending rights review:** Peng’s arXiv distribution license does not grant downstream reuse; Noy’s accessible MIT author manuscript and inaccessible Science publisher page do not establish a passage-reuse license for the selected final article. Do not treat open readability as reuse permission.
- The Science access failure and eScholarship robot challenge are access-tool observations, not proof of universal unavailability. Noy’s final article and the MIT working paper are distinct versions with a material participant-count mismatch (453 versus 444); final full-text comparison remains blocked by publisher access in this audit.
- The note does not authorize Veda to ingest, store, display, or commercially use any source. A source-stated license is evidence of stated terms, not legal clearance for a particular deployment, excerpt length, third-party material embedded in a paper, or all applicable rights. No full text was added to the repository.

## Readiness disposition

- **Identity/version/access metadata checked:** all six unique DOI targets were matched to the frozen rows; the arXiv records, INFORMS publisher records/PDFs, and Oxford Academic article were accessible in this recheck.
- **Do not yet treat as licensed passage source:** Peng, because the inspected arXiv grant only states a distribution right to arXiv; Noy–Zhang, because the final Science version and its terms were not accessible/verified; Brynjolfsson et al., until Veda's applicable use is confirmed noncommercial or separate permission is obtained.
- **Explicit CC BY 4.0 stated by the current source record/PDF:** Becker v2, Dell’Acqua publisher version, and Otis publisher version. Apply attribution/license/change conditions and check embedded third-party material separately. This is a source-terms finding, not legal clearance.
- **Pilot not ready for a quality claim:** even the sources with explicit CC BY 4.0 have not been validated by two independent qualified reviewers as a case-specific gold-source set; the frozen candidate mapping is not a gold answer. Noy remains version-blocked, and SUP01/SUP02 remain one shared study rather than independent replication.

The registry's prose citation for Dell’Acqua abbreviates “Artificial Intelligence” as “AI”; the DOI and ID establish identity, but that metadata discrepancy should be normalized in a future registry edit without changing the case sample. The evaluation README's pending-source-review wording should distinguish this completed metadata/version/rights screen from the still-pending independent source-set and reviewer validation. No full-text file was added to the repository.
